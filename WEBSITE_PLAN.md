# Personal Website Plan

This file tracks the structure, content inventory, and future features for the site.
It is intentionally separate from the public site so placeholder ideas do not crowd the live pages.

## Working Direction

- Tone: semi-professional, academic-leaning, warm, and slightly personal
- Structure: organized by content type
- Homepage role: likely archive-first with a strong identity-first introduction
- Course work: keep `Courses` easy to find during the semester

## Likely Top-Level Navigation

- Home
- About
- Projects
- Research
- Theses
- Papers
- Posts
- Documents
- Courses
- Contact

## Content Inventory

### Ready or Partly Ready

- GitHub
- LinkedIn
- Undergraduate thesis
- Resume(s)
- CV(s)
- Course projects for CSC 391

### Planned but Not Ready Yet

- Name pronunciation audio clips
- Expanded About page copy
- Posts / blog entries
- Papers page content curation
- Research notes and writeups
- Thesis presentation details

## Homepage Ideas

### Likely Structure

1. Short introduction with full name
2. Brief summary paragraph
3. Quick links to key sections
4. Featured items
5. Short site map into the archive

### Candidate Featured Items

- Undergraduate thesis
- Best project
- Most relevant paper or expository piece
- Courses / CSC 391 during the semester

## Open Questions

- Final homepage emphasis: identity-first or archive-first balance
- Whether `Theses` should remain a separate top-level page or live under `Research`
- Whether `Contact` should be a full page or a lighter section
- How much of the interests list should appear on `Home` versus `About`
- Which published items are ready enough to feature first

## Future Features

- Pronunciation buttons for full name
- Tags for projects, papers, and research topics
- Search or filtering if the site grows substantially
- Distinct cards or listings for theses, papers, and posts
- Better cross-linking between projects, papers, and research threads
- Download links for multiple document versions

## Notes

- Avoid publishing empty sections just to reserve space.
- Prefer private planning here, and publish a section once it has at least a real stub, description, or item.
- Keep the professor path simple: `Home -> Courses -> CSC 391 -> Project`.
